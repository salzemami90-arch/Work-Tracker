03

Two main places to work

Overview

Projects · Active Goals · Today · This Week · Upcoming and Overdue Deadlines · Achievements. Add a project or add a goal to a selected project. Keep completed steps separate from completed goals.

Project Details

Project status · Latest Summary · Next Action · Ordered Goals and Steps · Activity · My Notes. Expand goal details when needed and return easily to Overview.

Visual reference: dark navy sidebar, pale gray canvas, white cards, and muted green accents. Original Work Tracker styling; approved behavior unchanged. Laptop-first, English by default, with an Arabic interface option. User-entered content stays in its original language.

04

Flexible work, with a durable record

Projects and goals

Create and edit projects, goals, and steps without predefined software stages or assistant metadata. Complete one goal and add another within the same continuing project. Pausing preserves the project and its reason.

Acceptance: complete a goal without closing its project; inspect a paused project and its history.
Steps that can change

Add unexpected work to the appropriate goal. Complete, defer, reschedule, or move a step between goals within the same project. The moved step retains its identity and history. Reasons and helper names are optional.

Completing a goal warns about unfinished steps but never completes them automatically. A move alone is not an achievement. Remove from plan archives a step while preserving its dates, notes, and achievements; Restore to plan brings it back. The user chooses how many goals and steps to create.

Acceptance: add or move work without losing an earlier completion or its date.
Summaries, issues, and history

Retain successive summaries, independent updates, issues, plan changes, and completions with their dates. Keep the latest summary and next action easy to find. Optional links connect an update to a goal or step.

History remains after a month away. It supports reviewing when a problem was recorded; it does not diagnose the cause automatically.

Acceptance: read two assistant summaries and an independent update without one replacing another.
My Notes

A dated space for opinions, ideas, and reminders inside each project. No goal, step, or assistant is required. A new note does not erase an earlier one or create a task or achievement. Goals and steps also have their own dated Notes & updates for what happened, problems encountered, and decisions. These records remain visible in project history.

Acceptance: save two personal notes in a project with no goals, return, and read both.
Visible achievements

Record step and goal achievements separately. A step counts even when its goal is still active. Reopening preserves the earlier completion event; count each entity at most once per achievement period and indicate reopened work.

No invented overall completion percentage for ongoing projects. Planned work and completed work are clearly different.

05

Unfinished work stays in view

Today and This Week

Today includes pending work planned for today or earlier. This Week includes pending work through Saturday, including earlier weeks. Show Carried over and the original date; keep it until completion, deferral, or explicit rescheduling.

Plans and deadlines are different

A planned date says when to work; a deadline says when something is due. Both are optional. Deferral clears the planned date but keeps the deadline. Undated work is not automatically overdue.

Weeks run Sunday–Saturday. Upcoming covers today and the following six days. Passing midnight or a week boundary does not rewrite stored dates or create history events.

Accepted: paused-project visibility

Exclude paused-project steps from Today/This Week and their goals from Active Goals. Keep the project, notes, history, and achievements available. Deadlines remain visible with a Paused label. Resume using original dates, including carried-over work; no step status or date is rewritten. See the accepted technical rule.

06

Keep the sequence, not only the latest status

Illustrative sequence only. These are not preloaded tasks.

  1. A step is completedThe achievement keeps its completion date.
  2. New work is discoveredAdd another step to the chosen goal with an optional reason.
  3. The plan changesMove or defer work while preserving its previous context.
  4. The user returns laterRead what happened, where the project stopped, and what comes next.
07

Clear boundaries and reliable saving

Empty states and errors

First use invites a new project. Empty goals, steps, notes, or achievements do not produce fake data. Require titles, retain input on validation/save errors, and leave saved content unchanged when an edit is cancelled.

Local data and recovery

Save inside the project folder. A real process restart must retain data. Export and restore all projects, goals, steps, notes, summaries, and history. Detailed restore protections are proposed in the technical plan.

Outside the first version

No automatic assistant integrations, generated summaries, phone synchronization, accounts, or teams. No execution of the tracked business work. Arbitrary pasted text is not automatically converted into tasks.

08

Approved product requirements

The baseline and subsequent requested refinements are captured in the full requirements. Paused-project visibility has been accepted. The technical blueprint is approved and ready for 5-build.
